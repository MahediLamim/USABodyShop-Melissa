What We Do

Every repair under one roof

Body work and mechanical service in the same shop — you don't need a second stop or a second estimate.

Collision Repair

Full structural and cosmetic repair after an accident, from minor dents to major impact damage.

Frame Straightening

Precision measuring and pulling to bring a bent frame back to manufacturer specification.

Custom & Factory Paint

Computer-matched factory finishes and full custom paint work with a clean, even final coat.

Engine Diagnostics

Computerized diagnostics to find the actual cause of a warning light — not just clear the code.

Transmission Service

Fluid service, diagnosis, and repair for rough shifting, slipping, and transmission warning lights.

Brake Service

Pads, rotors, calipers, and brake lines — inspected and replaced before they become a safety issue.

Suspension Repair

Shocks, struts, and steering components fixed to correct pulling, bouncing, and uneven tire wear.

A/C & Heating

HVAC diagnosis, recharge, and component repair so your system holds up through a Texas summer.

Electrical Diagnostics

Batteries, alternators, starters, and wiring faults traced down properly instead of guessed at.

General Maintenance

Oil changes, fluids, filters, and scheduled service to keep small problems from getting expensive.
